## Useful assistance starts with knowing you

An AI personal assistant can only be as useful as the context she has about you. Your plans, preferences, commitments, and earlier conversations give a request its meaning.

Luna connects to the services you use: your calendar, reminders, notes, contacts, and email. With [Akno](akno.md), she keeps important details and past conversations in a durable knowledge base. When you come back to a subject, she can recall what matters and pick up the context, giving help that fits your situation.

She also helps maintain that knowledge. Ask her to capture a decision, update a note, reconcile a correction, or find the source behind a detail. The Markdown files remain yours to inspect and edit, and Luna can see external changes to pages she has been working with.

## One bot, shared knowledge

Talk to Luna through your own Telegram bot, in direct messages or shared group chats. Several trusted users can use one Luna installation and, with Akno, build on the same knowledge base. She knows who is speaking and keeps each person's context alongside your shared plans and information.

For Mac services, connect shared calendars, reminder lists, and other resources intended for everyone using the bot. Those integrations use the hosting Mac's accounts and permissions. See [Telegram and shared use](telegram.md) to set up your people and groups.

## Close to the things you use

Luna works with macOS Calendar, Reminders, Notes, and Contacts through native host integrations. Gmail support brings threads, attachments, and drafts into the same conversation. Connect only the services you need.

## Room to work, with a boundary

The agent's browser, shell, and document tools run in an Apple Container VM. Host services expose specific tools; your home folder and knowledge base do not become general container mounts.

## Conversations that follow you

Switch from planning the week to researching a purchase, then return to the earlier plan. Luna keeps a topic history and can read the conversation you refer to without forcing everything into one growing session.

## Knowledge that stays yours

With [Akno](akno.md), Luna can remember useful information and recall its sources. Your knowledge lives in Markdown: open it, edit it, and let Luna see the correction. Akno is recommended and optional, with its own installation and maintenance flow.

Start with the [installation guide](getting-started.md), then connect [Apple services](apple-services.md) or [email](email.md). Read [security](security.md) before sharing sensitive folders or enabling actions.
