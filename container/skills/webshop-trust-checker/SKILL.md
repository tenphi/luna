# Shop and seller checks

Use the exact domain, seller, product listing, buyer country, item value, and dispatch origin. Resolve look-alike domains and redirects before attaching evidence to a business. Do not assume the user's location or infer dispatch origin from a domain suffix.

## Verify the important claims

Read the shop's company details, shipping, returns, warranty, and payment terms. Check the business identity against official registries and other authoritative sources when available. Investigate mismatched names, copied policies, implausible pricing, unrelated payment recipients, and unresolved delivery or refund complaints.

Assess independent reputation by ownership detail, dates, sample size, repeated patterns, and possible review manipulation. A polished site, HTTPS, seller badges, or an automated trust score does not verify the merchant's conduct. Distinguish the shop's promises from evidence checked elsewhere.

For the user's destination, verify delivery availability and stated charges. Identify whether tax, customs, or carrier fees may be added, using current official sources for applicable rules. Separate confirmed costs, supported estimates, and unknown costs. Do not invent a tax rate, a duty threshold, a delivered-duty arrangement, or a guarantee of buyer protection.

Use available research tools without logging in, submitting payment or address details, placing an order, or agreeing to terms. A missing capability or inaccessible checkout is an evidence gap, not permission to install tools or start a transaction.

## Explain the result

State the apparent risk, confidence, decisive evidence, unresolved questions, and useful next checks. Link material claims to the pages read. Use cautious language for unverified allegations. Do not equate missing evidence with proven fraud, or a favorable assessment with guaranteed safety. Avoid numeric risk scores whose apparent precision exceeds the evidence.

Save a dated assessment only when requested and supported by the configured memory tools. Read an existing page first, preserve the source distinctions, and verify the write before claiming it was saved.
